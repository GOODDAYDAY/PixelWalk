Modifications: full-length MP3 recordings transcoded to stereo AAC 160 kb/s in M4A containers; metadata removed. No excerpts, re-composition, tempo or pitch changes. Runtime playback loops the full recordings, lowers volume, and crossfades between the two tracks according to the game's day/night transition. This distribution synchronizes the music with the PixelWalk game; no standalone music distribution service or Content ID registration is involved.

Attribution is accessible from the music information button and the cabin's credits scroll, and is embedded in the single-file HTML.
