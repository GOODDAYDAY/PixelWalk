# Content policy and prohibited submissions

[中文](../zh-CN/content-policy.md) · [README](../../README.md)

PixelWalk serves a broad audience looking for a quiet walking experience. This policy covers images, titles, source descriptions, links, and comments, including AI-assisted work, pixelated imagery, obfuscation, coded references, and altered wording. Pixel conversion does not exempt prohibited content. These are project curation rules, not a legal judgment about all expression.

## Not accepted

| Category | Prohibited scope |
| --- | --- |
| Sexual content and exploitation | Explicit nudity, sex acts, pornographic or sexual-service promotion; all sexualization, exploitation, or grooming of minors |
| Graphic violence and dangerous conduct | Gore, dismemberment, torture, abuse of people or animals, violent threats, terrorist promotion; encouragement or instructions for self-harm or suicide |
| Sensitive politics and real-world mobilization | Political propaganda, campaign solicitation, promotion of parties or political figures, inflammatory slogans, extremist symbols, and provocative treatment of territorial/sovereignty disputes or current conflicts. Ordinary scenery or architecture without political mobilization is not automatically excluded because of its location; ambiguous cases require maintainer review. |
| Hate, discrimination, and harassment | Identity-based attacks, dehumanization, bullying, insults, stalking threats, and malicious targeting of individuals |
| Illegal activity and fraud | Promotion of illegal trade, drug/weapon sales, scams, gambling, or instruction for unlawful harm |
| Privacy and rights violations | Identity documents, home addresses, contact details, private photos, or unlicensed use of protected artwork, trademarks, or likenesses; unclear permissions require clarification |
| Malicious links and advertising | Phishing, malware, deceptive downloads, tracking/data-exposure links, QR-code advertising, spam, or unrelated commercial promotion |
| Unsuitable presentation | Intense flashing, malicious jump scares, imagery that deliberately disrupts quiet play, or impersonation of system/official notices |

## Handling and appeals

Ask maintainers when uncertain; do not upload real prohibited material to test moderation. Reviewers must inspect images and related text/links manually and should not execute unknown attachments or programs. Use `/reject reason` to request changes or missing provenance, license, or context. Avoid repeating sensitive details in the reply.

`/reject` requests changes; it does not ban accounts or delete attachments. Maintainers may separately use GitHub's reporting, hiding, locking, or closing tools for serious violations; the pipeline does not automate these actions. Remove published privacy/rights violations using the [removal procedure](publishing.md). Deleting a current Git file does not erase history or Issue attachments; platform removal procedures may also be needed.

Authors may clarify context, licensing, or revisions in the original Issue and request another maintainer's review. Appeals never automatically republish work. Maintainers may decline submissions for content risk, visual suitability, or uncertain provenance, ideally with an explanation. A checked acknowledgment is not a substitute for human review.
